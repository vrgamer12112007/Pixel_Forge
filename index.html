Ask your question →
How much does a website cost?
Most projects land between ₹49,000 and ₹2,50,000 depending on scope. A starter 5-page site is ₹49,000; a full clinic or gym site with online booking is ₹1,20,000; the premium package with automation starts at ₹2,50,000. After our free call, you get a fixed, itemized quote — no surprise invoices, ever.
How long does a project take?
A starter site: 2 weeks. A growth site with booking: 4–6 weeks. A premium site with automation: 6–10 weeks. We share weekly progress previews so you always know exactly where things stand.
I'm a doctor / gym owner. Can you build for my industry?
That's our specialty. We've designed demo sites for clinics, gyms, and salons specifically because we understand the conversion patterns, compliance needs, and booking flows that work in those industries. Check our work section to see live demos for each.
Do you handle the domain and hosting?
Yes — we'll set up your domain (or migrate an existing one), configure hosting on Vercel or your preferred provider, and handle SSL, DNS, and email setup. You own all accounts; we just configure them for you.
Will I own the website after launch?
Completely. You own 100% of the codebase, Figma designs, domain, hosting, and content. We hand over a private GitHub repo and Figma transfer on launch day. No vendor lock-in, no hostage situations.
What is workflow automation and do I need it?
Workflow automation connects your website to tools like WhatsApp, your CRM, and your calendar — so bookings, reminders, follow-ups, and invoices happen automatically. We recommend it as a phase 2 add-on after your website is built. Most businesses don't need it from day one.
What if I don't like the design?
Our design phase includes two structured revision rounds built into the timeline, and we share weekly previews so feedback lands early. If we ever miss the mark after revisions, we'll redesign until you're happy — or refund your money. That's our promise.
Can you fix my existing website?
Yes. We offer a free website audit covering performance, SEO, accessibility, and conversion. From there you get a prioritized fix list — and you decide whether to fix in place or rebuild from scratch.
